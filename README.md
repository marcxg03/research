# Research

Index of my research software and analysis pipelines — psychology research tooling built as an RA at the **Oishi Lab (University of Chicago)** and for related collaborations. Each project lives in its own repo; this page tracks what exists, what it does, and where it stands.

## Projects

| Project | What it is | Status | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| [epiphany_scraper](https://github.com/marcxg03/epiphany_scraper) | Narrative example-bank retrieval tool for epiphanic narrative sequence research (primary project; poster presented Apr 2026) | Active | Public |

## Themes

- **Narrative identity** — epiphanic sequences, narrative example banks, passage extraction from natural text.
- **Data quality** — bot detection and participant screening for online survey research.
- **Computational cultural psychology** — geographic corpora, embeddings, cultural dimension projection.
- **Research operations** — AI-augmented lab workflows and automation.
